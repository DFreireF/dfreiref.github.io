##### Steps

1. Heat water to 92°C.
2. Place the V60 on your mug and add a paper filter.
3. Rinse the filter with hot water.
4. Add ground coffee and let it bloom for 30 seconds.
5. Pour water slowly in a circular motion.
6. Enjoy your coffee!
